On 2026-10-08 the user liked a step-by-step explanation of how to evaluate SFT data, but said: "i want without any analogies, i hate those". The explanation had used a student / textbook / exam metaphor.

**Why:** they want the real mechanism in the real terms (model, dataset, benchmark, run), not a story they then have to translate back.

**How to apply:**
- Explain in detail, step by step, in simple English, using the actual technical words. Define a word the first time it appears instead of replacing it with a metaphor.
- Keep the step-by-step structure, the small worked examples and the real numbers. Drop phrases like "like a student", "good day / bad day", "chapters".
