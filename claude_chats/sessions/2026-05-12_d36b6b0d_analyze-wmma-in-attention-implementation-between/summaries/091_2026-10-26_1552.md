**2. Key Technical Concepts**

*Generic attention*
- Generic GQA WMMA kernels with a `CAUSAL` template flag, using the per-segment list `gen_segs {b, t0, L, 0}` (FA2 varlen `cu_seqlens` style, "design A").
- Tiles counted from the chat start, so packed == alone bit-exact.
- Known precision issue: dS computed from bf16 P.

*Kernel dispatch and build*
- `get_arch()` → `ArchFamily`.
- cp.async needs sm_80+; TMA needs sm_90+.
- The `-MMD -MP` dependency build exists.

*Matmul audit concepts*
- GEMM fp32-accumulator epilogue fusion.
- `grad_view` bf16 wire buffer.
- `blublasBgemmAddmm` SM90 epilogue with bias_numel {1, N, M, M·N}.
- Unwired fused SwiGLU GEMM.

*CUTLASS SM90 schedules*
- **Cooperative:** 2 consumer warpgroups on ONE tile, split along M.
- **Pingpong (`sm90_gemm_tma_warpspecialized_pingpong.hpp`):**
  - 1 load warpgroup + 2 MMA warpgroups, 384 threads;
  - registers 40/232 (24/240 under heavy register pressure, when RegsPerThread ≥ 208);
  - `MathWarpGroupOrderBarrier = OrderedSequenceBarrier<2, 2>`, with the comments "Order two Math WG's MMA one after the other, helps hide Epilogue" and "Order two Math WG's Epilogue one after the other";
  - Consumer1 advances one tile at startup (static persistent scheduler); then `advance_to_next_work(NumMmaWarpGroups)`;
  - the MMA-order arrive happens after `mma()` and before `mma_tail`.

*FA3 §3.1 "pingpong scheduling"*
- `bar.sync` between warpgroups so one warpgroup's softmax overlaps the other's GEMMs (attention).
- This is a different use of the word from the GEMM schedule.
- Our attention v62 baton also does this.

*"Buffer ping-pong" (double buffering, e.g. `EPI_WL_STAGES=2`, `UR32 ±0x1000`)*
- Unrelated to scheduling.

*cuBLAS nvjet names*
- coopA / coopB / no tag.
- H23d/H24 inferred the untagged family = ping-pong from register evidence:
  - max register = blocks/WG × wgmmaN/2 + 26, exact on 12/12 kernels;
  - the barrier test was inconclusive;
  - no SETMAXNREG in cuBLAS;
  - CUTLASS issue #2181 is unanswered.
