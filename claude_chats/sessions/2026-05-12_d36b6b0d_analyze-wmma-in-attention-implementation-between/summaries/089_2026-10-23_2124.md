2. Key Technical Concepts:

**SFT run and continuation**
- SFT resume from DCP checkpoints (metadata.bin + data_rank_0.bin). Checkpoints are saved at (step+1)%1000 and at the final step. With BLU_CKPT_KEEP=1 the trainer saves first and then prunes `blumodel_step_*`.
- The 4x box defaults to CUDA 13.4, so the run put the 13.0 libs first in LD_LIBRARY_PATH.
- Trainer backward is non-determinism (resume drift ≤1.3e-4).

**Model mapped to HF Qwen3ForCausalLM**
- Per-head QK-norm gamma applied before NeoX RoPE.
- Linear weights are stored [in,out] and must be transposed for HF.
- `gate_up` is [gate|up] and is split into two weights.
- Param order is: own params first (q_gamma, k_gamma), then children (norm, wQ, wK, wV, wO), then the MLP (norm, gate_up, down), then wte and norm_f.

**Evaluation**
- lm-eval 0.4.13 with transformers 5.20.2: use `dtype=` (not `torch_dtype`), and do not put `max_new_tokens` in generation_config.
- Chat-template vs plain format: DROP stops at "." and multiple-choice tasks get depressed in chat format.
- Paired per-question test (z score).
- MMLU-Pro random subset via `--samples`.

**Memorization measure**
- Epoch-2 step 5795+n shares 16 of 20 rows with epoch-1 step n.
- Memorization = (same-rows train-loss drop) − (val drop).
- Nats = −ln(p).

**Hyperparameters**
- Rows per step = B × ranks × GRAD_ACCUM.
- Memory ≈ fixed + B × per-row.

**RoPE bit-identity and speed**
- `--use_fast_math` changes FMA pairing, which broke bit-identity at hd64 in the pipelined backward.
- Our backward runs at low occupancy (1-warp blocks, 128 tokens per warp).
- torch.compile's Triton backward reaches 72–79% of DRAM bandwidth.
