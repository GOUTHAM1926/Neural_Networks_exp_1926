2. Key Technical Concepts:
   - **Hardware and kernel:** H200 sm_90a persistent attention forward v62. Two consumer warpgroups in ping-pong (baton named barriers 1/2), one producer warp.
     - TMA loads with mbarrier full/empty rings (3 stages, K+V combined), qfull/qempty.
     - K4 O-staging in the last K buffer; kcount handshake; order_after trick (data-dependent mbarrier arrive pins the P·V wait).
   - **Power cap:** every run sits at the 700 W cap (691–695 W).
     - Forward-only races let the forward set its own clock; the fwd+bwd regime (scoreboard and trainer) shares the clock.
     - Energy savings raise the clock; cycle savings convert fully in fwd+bwd. Unchanged kernels shift ±0.2–0.6% via the shared clock.
     - On this box ncu does NOT lock one clock (1.63–1.74 GHz per kernel).
   - **ncu PC sampling** (warp stall sampling per instruction):
     - Stall reasons used: wait, dispatch, not_selected, short_sb, long_sb, barrier, and an unlabeled one = gmma back-pressure.
     - BAR.SYNC.DEFER_BLOCKING: the baton wait shows as "barrier" samples on the next instructions.

     - L2 hints: K/V EVICT_LAST 0x14F0000000000000, Q EVICT_FIRST 0x12F0000000000000.
     - softmax.h: thread_reduce_ = one max chain per row; scale_apply_exp2 row by row; reduce_sum after the exps.
     - mask.h: row by row, constant column vs a per-row limit.
     - Producer order: Q L2 prefetch (:668), load_K (:769), QueryEmpty (:776), Q (:780).
