2. Key Technical Concepts:
   - **CP (context parallelism) Ulysses:**
     - combine all-to-all: [B,H,T/U,D] → [B,H/U,T,D]; partition is its inverse.
     - world = DP × CP(U). The CP group is U consecutive ranks reading the same rows; DP groups read different rows.
   - **Our CP design per layer:** projections on the slice → our fused_rms_rope_bf16 (QK-norm+RoPE, per-chat positions, 2 outputs: q_bf output 0, k_bf output 1) → his combine → our packed attention (chat plan built for the full row with Nq/U heads) → his partition.
   - **Adhitya's design:** QK-norm+RoPE inside his attention kernel after the exchange (positions 0..T−1); his q/k/v come from autograd::transpose and enter as fp32 (to_master).
   - **Loss:** ours is sum over the slice's trained tokens / global count (count summed over all GPUs ÷ U), seed = world size, ZeRO average → effectively a sum. His is the mean per GPU, seed 1/GA, ZeRO average (wrong weighting for SFT).
   - **His gamma rule:** QK-norm after the exchange → head-partitioned gamma grads → all-reduce op_t::avg (pre-division). It doesn't apply to us (our QK-norm is before the exchange, token-partitioned).
   - **TI autograd bug** (Tensor::contiguous returns *this; make_unary_op stamps the grad_fn onto the same object; output_nr unchanged → edge to a nonexistent slot → ContiguousBackward::apply returns empty → the dK gradient is dropped).
   - **Embedding backward:** atomic vs cooperative vs segmented (BluCCL embedding_fused.cu, production YAML `embedding.backward: "segmented"`).
     - TI d067e89 swapped the atomic kernel's grid order → 5.8× slower on real text (repeated tokens) for trainers without the segmented switch (Navin's 135M eqab trainer).
   - **Blocking CUDA stream** for the CP group (ordered after legacy stream 0); Work::wait() = cudaStreamWaitEvent (GPU-side).
   - The autograd engine runs sequentially (FIFO queue, dependencies counted per edge).
   - Measurement method: a 30-step CSV stop, median of steps 11–29, holder locks, a smoke gate.
