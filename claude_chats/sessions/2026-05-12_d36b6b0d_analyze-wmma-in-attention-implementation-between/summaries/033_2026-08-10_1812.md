- wgmma fp32 accumulator fragment layout (from `cute::SM90::GMMA::CLayout_64xN`): `row = t1 + 8*v1 + 16*t2`, `col = 2*t0 + v0 + 8*v2`
- `cp.async.bulk.wait_group.read N` — "wait until at most Count committed TMA_STOREs are pending"; `.read` = smem source consumed
- `fence.proxy.async.shared::cta` (tma_store_fence), `cp.async.bulk.commit_group`
- CUTLASS `EpilogueTileAuto`: `tile_m=min(128,CTA_M)`, `tile_n=gcd(min(32,CTA_N),CTA_N)`, `StagesD=min(EpiTiles,2)`
- DeepGEMM `SMEM_CD_SIZE = STORE_BLOCK_M * (kSwizzleCDMode/sizeof(dtype)) * kNumTMAStoreStages`
- `stmatrix.sync.aligned.x4.m8n8.shared.b16` (SM90_U32x4_STSM_N)
- nvjet kernel naming: `nvjet_sm90_tst_<BM>x<BN>_<BK>x<STAGES>_<CX>x<CY>_<h|v>_bz_[coopA|coopB|splitK]_<SFX>`
