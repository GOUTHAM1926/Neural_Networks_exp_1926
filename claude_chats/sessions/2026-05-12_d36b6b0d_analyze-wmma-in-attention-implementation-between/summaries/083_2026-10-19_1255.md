2. Key Technical Concepts:
   - **Hardware and kernels:** H200 sm_90a; persistent attention forward v62 (K5q = K4b + softmax split 16 + one P register set). Two consumer warpgroups in ping-pong (named-barrier baton pp_sync/pp_arrive), one producer warp (TMA), mbarrier full/empty rings (NSTAGES=3, K+V combined 64 KB), qfull/qempty, sched_full (unit hand-over), order_bar trick (data-dependent arrive pins the P·V wait), K4b (O staged in the last K buffer, kcount monotonic counter handshake), design A packed units (inactive tail for q-tiles past a segment end).
   - **FA3 (flash-attention 94e22c9 hopper/) for head dim 128 causal:**
     - tile 128×128, MmaPV_is_RS, IntraWGOverlap, kStages=2;
     - separate MainloopPipelineK/V;
     - loader order K(n) then V(n−1);
     - WG0 waits K before QK and V(prev) before PV;
     - K released right after QK (mainloop:1185), V after PV (1199).
   - **Measurement method:**
     - ncu tensor-busy/idle cycles;
     - fit of idle = a×units + b×tiles;
     - per-step timeline traces (TLK5q clock stamps; the trace bookkeeping adds ~250 cycles);
     - warp-stall shares ≠ tensor idle (lesson from L1b);
     - knock-out builds (measurement only);
     - races in ab_multi.sh (quick suite 296 checks incl. packed == alone bit-exact, rotating order, paired t-stats via race_stats.py);
     - free-clock scoreboard under nsys (kernel times, ABBA order, steady state after 2 s).
   - **ptxas behaviour:**
     - it rematerializes "computed once" uniform values; a `__shfl_sync` makes them opaque (L1b);
     - CUDA 13.4 compiles K5q differently (QK issue dense 59, packed 40 with 8 descriptor groups).
   - **MUFU:** one exp keeps the exp unit busy ~8 cycles per warp; FA3 interleaves row-2 max with row-1 exps.
   - **Loss kernel:**
     - OwnTensor::bfloat16_t has a user copy constructor (Types.h:451), so it is not trivially copyable, so a 16-B struct of 8 is copied as 8 × 2-B accesses;
     - `__nv_bfloat16` is trivially copyable (1 × 16-B);
     - the library rule `CudaNativeType<T>` (include/dtype/CudaTraits.h:17-22) is used by reduction but not by the loss module.
   - **Protocol model:** a CPU model of the barrier protocol (protocol_sim_skv.py) with deadlock + corruption checks (read at issue, read at release, O staging) and negative tests.
