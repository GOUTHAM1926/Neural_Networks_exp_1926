2. **Key Technical Concepts**

   - **split_turns cases**
     1. Fits.
     2. Cut between turns.
     3. Cut inside the answer, with the full question repeated.
     5. Question over the ctx_frac budget:
        - 5a: question fits a row with ≥ SPLIT_MIN_FIRST = 64 answer ids after it.
        - 5b: question longer than that.
     - "after5" = turns after the first 5a/5b turn.
   - **Hidden copy**
     - Definition: an answer n-gram (8 ids, also checked with 4) that appears earlier in the chat (question or earlier turns) but is not in the piece before that position.
     - Scored as hidden copies per 1k trained tokens. It measures training the model to write text it cannot see.
     - Reference levels: the pushed split has 25.6 (case-2 turns) and 33.6 (case 3); TRL bfd_split is up to 103.6.
   - **Layouts** studied: end, head+tail, select (answer-chosen 256-id chunks by 8-gram overlap), selht (first 64 + chosen chunks + last 192), fullq, fill.
   - **History context**: whole turns (pushed), against answer-chosen chunks up to a quarter row (select).
   - **Per-turn filter**: `drop_hidden_turns` at max_hidden 100.
   - **Mechanics**:
     - n-gram hashing with uint64 wrapping arithmetic.
     - Chunk selection: ties go to the later chunk; the last chunk keeps its end.
     - Pieces are copies of ids in chat order; true predecessor; `seq_complete.bin`; no end token at cuts.
   - **Instruction placement in the data**: LongAlign = document then the question at the end; WildChat = instruction at the start or the end.
   - **Llama 3 §4.3.4**: 0.1% synthetic long-context SFT data.
