2. Key Technical Concepts:

   **Attention forward v62 on H200 sm_90a**
   - Structure: persistent kernel, 2 consumer warpgroups in ping-pong with baton named barriers, 1 producer warp, TMA, a 3-stage K/V ring, K4 O-staging, kcount.
   - Region accounting from ncu PC sampling: share of all samples × SM cycles / 34.9 units.
   - Each mbarrier wait is costed at its retry branch (`@!P BRA` after `SYNCS.PHASECHK.TRANS64.TRYWAIT`).

   **FA3 mechanisms (flash-attention 94e22c9)**
   - Q L2 prefetch at `mainloop_fwd_sm90_tma_gmma_ws.hpp:681`.
   - QueryEmpty release at :1237; masked FIRST tile at :1150.
   - Epilogue: one TMA-store warp, the last warp of the second warpgroup, 288-thread named barrier (`epilogue_fwd.hpp` :249-327); `barrier_O` waited before V (:865).

   **Candidate switches** (`patches/make_pfq.py`)
   - `Q_PREF`: `cp.async.bulk.prefetch.tensor.2d.L2.global`.
   - `DEC_PUB`: the producer publishes `b`, `hq`, `pair` in `sched_dec[3*(k&1)]`, read at the unit top.
   - `DIAG_FIRST`: the producer loads tile `nkv-1-n`; the prologue masks.

   **Loop gate:** the steady loop must stay SASS-identical (`loop_sass.py`, `loop_order_diff.py` with canonical register renaming). ptxas reorders cost PFT +40 cycles/step.

   **Power cap 700 W:** cycle savings convert to time only partially (QPU: −1.51% cycles → −0.81% time).

   **Loss kernels**
   - `bfloat16_t` has a user-written copy ctor, so `LossVec<T>` copies compiled to 2-byte loads. The fix is `loss_ld16`/`loss_st16` through `uint4`.
   - `CudaNativeType<T>` lives in `include/dtype/CudaTraits.h:22` and is not used in the loss files.

   **In-place gradient**
   - The loss backward writes dL/dlogits over the logits buffer (stats path).
   - Contract: nothing may read the logits after `backward()`.
