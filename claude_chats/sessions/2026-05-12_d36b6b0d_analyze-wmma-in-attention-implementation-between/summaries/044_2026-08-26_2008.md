## 2. Key Technical Concepts

- SM90 warp-specialized Hopper GEMM: 1 producer + 2 consumer warpgroups, TMA, wgmma/HGMMA, mbarriers, thread-block clusters
- **P = α·C·V²·f** — C is capacitance (fixed silicon), α is activity factor (workload-dependent). `f = P/(α·C·V²)` at a fixed power cap
- **Sum vs union**: sum double-counts concurrent work; union merges intervals. Difference = overlap
- **Occupancy**: our GEMM CTA = 229,376 B dynamic smem (100% of 228 KB) + 384×168 = 64,512 of 65,536 registers (98.4%), 132 CTAs / 132 SMs = 1 CTA/SM
- **Warp-uniform vs per-thread datapath**: `IMAD`/`IADD3`/`SHF`/`ISETP` (per-thread) vs `UIMAD`/`UIADD3`/`USHF`/`UISETP`/`ULEA` (uniform, once per warp). `R2UR` = crossing from per-thread to uniform register
- **`F2FP.BF16.F32.PACK_AB`** — the fp32→bf16 packed conversion, identical in ours and cuBLAS SASS
- **`HGMMA.*.F32.BF16`** — bf16 operands, fp32 accumulator; ISA offers no f16-accumulate variant for bf16
- ncu `--section SourceCounters` + `--page source --print-source cuda,sass --csv` gives **dynamic per-SASS-line execution counts** with source correlation (needs `-lineinfo`)
- `-lineinfo` does NOT change codegen (verified: 1023 SASS lines before and after)
- `clocks_event_reasons.*` (driver 580 renamed from `clocks_throttle_reasons.*`)
