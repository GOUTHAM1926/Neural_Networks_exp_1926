2. Key Technical Concepts:

**Vj2 backward kernel (FA3-style, opt-in `USE_VJ2=1`).**
- Structure: a loader warpgroup, 2 compute warpgroups, wgmma (HGMMA) SS/RS, `wgmma_wait1`/`wgmma_wait0`, warpgroup arrive stalls, a named barrier BAR_PDS before the dQ GEMM.
- dQ path: a fragment-order fp32 dQ accumulator plus bulk reduce-add, then the `convert_dq_frag_vj2` kernel.
- Prep: the `bwd_prep_vj2` kernel (Drow, Lse2).

**ncu stall reasons.** short_scoreboard (waiting on LDS or MUFU results), mio_throttle, barrier (includes DEPBAR), warpgroup_arrive (= the "unexplained" samples). µs-equivalent = share of samples × kernel time.
