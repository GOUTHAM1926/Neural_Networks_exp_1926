2. Key Technical Concepts:
   - SM90 warp-specialized Hopper GEMM: 1 producer + 2 consumer warpgroups (384 threads), TMA, wgmma/HGMMA, mbarriers, persistent grid-stride scheduling
   - Sm90WorkGrid: layout-aware derivation of WGMMA_N, work-grid block-count parity (EVEN=coopA-like, ODD=coopB-like in cuBLAS's own SASS, though this is NOT our own naming)
   - GMMA descriptor bitfield (`cute::GmmaDescriptor` in `cute/arch/mma_sm90_desc.hpp`): `start_address_` (14 bits, [0,14), 4-LSB-truncated/16-byte-aligned), `leading_byte_offset_` (14 bits), `stride_byte_offset_` (14 bits), `base_offset_` (3 bits, [49,52), valid only for SWIZZLE_128B/64B), `layout_type_` (2 bits: NONE=0,B128=1,B64=2,B32=3)
