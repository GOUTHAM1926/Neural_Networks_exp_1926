2. Key Technical Concepts:

- **H200 basics.** Hopper sm_90a: wgmma (HGMMA) and TMA (cp.async.bulk.tensor), mbarriers (phase/parity try_wait), named barriers (ping-pong baton), setmaxnreg.
- **Persistent kernel, warp-specialized:** 1 producer warpgroup + 2 consumer warpgroups.
- **Forward v62:** BLOCK_M 64 per group, 128 keys, NSTAGES 3, units are query pairs.
- **FA3 IntraWGOverlap order:** QK(n) + P·V(n−1) issued together; wait for QK; softmax; wait for P·V; rescale; pack.
- **Free vs locked clocks.** The 700 W power cap; the clock depends on instruction mix and power.
- **Timing methods.**
  - Our C++ BENCH = kernel + ~10 µs.
  - The Python bench = kernel + 83–324 µs (biased).
  - nsys gives kernel-only times.
  - GPU-busy %.
- **Paired 12-round races** with rotating order; t-stats.
- **L2 cache hints:** `createpolicy.fractional.L2::evict_last/evict_first` + `.L2::cache_hint` on TMA loads.
- **Dynamic work order:** atomic counter, self-resetting; LPT sections of 48 heads (32 MB of K/V).
- **CPU protocol model for deadlock checking:** protocol_sim.py.
- **Register split:** FA3 uses 24/240; ours 40/232 is better.
