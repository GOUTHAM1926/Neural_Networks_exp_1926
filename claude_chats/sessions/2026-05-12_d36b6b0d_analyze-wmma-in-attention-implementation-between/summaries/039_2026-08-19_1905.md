5. **Problem Solving:**

**SOLVED — the big win.** `EPI_LATE_STORE_WAIT`: we drained the epilogue's TMA store at the END of every tile, but the next tile's mainloop never touches `c_buf` (`store_smem = smem_base + LOAD_PIPE_DEPTH * copy_size`, past every A/B stage). Moving the wait to the top of the next epilogue hides it behind a full tile of k-iterations.
